Valoria Studio Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025

1. Who is responsible

The Valoria Studio app (“App”) is developed and published by Wolfie Soft (“Author”). Privacy contact: wolfiestudio.apps@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The App does not require an account or sign-in.
  • The App contains no ads, analytics or tracking tools.
  • Your data (songs, songbooks, setlists, settings) is stored on your device. The Author has no access to it and the App does not send it to any server operated by the Author.
  • The App connects to the internet only when you use a feature that requires it (section 5).

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores locally the content you create or import: song lyrics and chords, songbooks, setlists, categories, tags, notes, imported files and settings. You can delete it at any time within the App, by clearing the App's data in system settings, or by uninstalling the App.

If Android backup is enabled on your device (e.g. Google Backup), the system may automatically back up the App's data to your backup account. This follows your device settings and the backup provider's terms – the Author has no access to these backups.

4. Permissions and their purpose

  • Microphone – used only while you use: the tuner and sound analysis tools, transcription (Whisper speech recognition runs entirely on your device), voice messages in the band chat, and dictation in the song editor. Dictation uses the system speech recognition service installed on your device (e.g. Google), which may process audio on its provider's servers under that provider's privacy policy.
  • Camera – to scan a QR code when joining a band room and to recognise text in photos (Google ML Kit, on-device). The App does not upload photos or camera images.
  • Files and media – to import and export files you choose (e.g. documents, PDF, MIDI, audio, images).
  • Bluetooth and local network – for band mode: direct communication with nearby devices (sharing songs, chat, voice messages, synchronisation). Data goes only to connected devices, without any server. On Android 11 and older the system requires the location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices – the App does not read or store your location.
  • Notifications – practice reminders and status of the metronome and band mode.
  • Clipboard – only to paste text when you ask for it (e.g. importing a song).

5. Internet connections

The App connects to external services only at your request:

  • Downloading speech recognition models from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). The server sees standard connection data (e.g. IP address).
  • Importing songs from websites in the built-in browser – visited websites may use cookies and have their own privacy policies.
  • YouTube player – showing videos linked to a song; the Google/YouTube privacy policy applies.
  • Opening links in an external browser.

These services are responsible for the data they process, e.g. Google Privacy Policy, Hugging Face Privacy Policy.

The bundled Google ML Kit library may send limited technical diagnostic data about the text recognition feature to Google (without photo contents or recognised text).

6. Feedback and bug reports

The “Send feedback” feature opens your e-mail app with a prepared message. In addition to the text you enter, it includes your Android version, device model and App version. You send the message yourself – the Author then receives your e-mail address and the report. They are used only to reply and resolve the issue (legal basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and are deleted at your request.

7. Data sharing

The Author does not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties.

8. Your rights

For data you have provided to the Author (e.g. by e-mail) you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (in Poland: President of the Personal Data Protection Office, uodo.gov.pl).

9. Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children and does not send any user's data to the Author.

10. Security

Data is stored in the App's private storage, protected by Android security mechanisms. Files you export or share outside the App are subject to the rules of the place where you save or send them.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated, e.g. when new features are added. The current version is always available in the App (About → Privacy policy) and at the address listed on Google Play. The effective date is shown at the top of this document.
